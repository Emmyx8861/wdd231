The Silver Lining Of Xavier Reeds: Prologue

Est. read time: 5 minutes (excerpt)

The abyss is dead. I killed it.
I tore it apart, watched it collapse, felt it vanish into nothing.
And yet…
I can still hear it.
It whispers in the dark, in the empty spaces between thoughts.
In the silence of forgotten places.
The abyss is dead.
But something remains.
And I am beginning to wonder—
Did I destroy it?
Or did I become it?
When Isabella fell, the universe held its breath.
The sky above the last world repaired itself.
The ground beneath our feet solidified.
The ruins of existence trembled—and then stilled.
The abyss was gone.
And yet, as I stood there, staring at the empty space where Isabella had once been—something felt wrong.
I felt wrong.
Because deep inside me, where the abyss had once burrowed—
Something was still there.
